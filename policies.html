Policies

How I work: ethics, privacy, bookings and safety

Last updated: 2 November 2026. These policies apply to every session, programme and enquiry with Rodrigo Soria (Ireland). They are written in plain language; if anything is unclear, ask before booking.

1. Professional conduct and scope of practice

  • I work as a Transpersonal Therapist, Shamanic Practitioner and Shamanic Counselor. My services are complementary and educational: they support self-understanding, rest and integration. They are not medical or psychological diagnosis or treatment, and they do not replace care from a doctor, psychologist, psychiatrist or other health professional.
  • I do not diagnose conditions and I do not claim to cure, treat or heal any illness. Nothing on this site or said in a session should be read as a promise of a specific outcome.
  • I only offer the practices I have trained in, and I will tell you when something you need is outside my competence and suggest more appropriate support.
  • Every session starts with a clear agreement: what we will do, how long it takes, the fee, confidentiality and how we close. You can pause, decline any practice or end a session at any moment, without having to justify it.
  • Boundaries are kept clear and respectful. I do not enter romantic, sexual or financial relationships with clients.
  • I keep my knowledge current through ongoing training and seek supervision or mentoring when a situation calls for it.

2. Privacy and data protection

I process personal data under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Irish Data Protection Acts. Rodrigo Soria is the data controller.

  • What I collect: what you send through the contact form (name, e-mail, phone if given, chosen service, message), the pre-requisite form (spiritual background, intention, allergies, medications and health conditions relevant to the work), scheduling details, and session notes.
  • Why: to answer you, to prepare and run your sessions safely, to keep the records a practitioner must keep, and to issue receipts. Health information is used only to adapt the work to you and is never shared for marketing.
  • Tools that handle your data: the contact form is delivered by Formspree; the pre-requisite form runs on Google Forms; online scheduling uses Cal.com; e-mail. Each provider processes data under its own privacy terms, and I only use what you submit.
  • Recordings: Shamanic Counseling and programme sessions are recorded in audio for you. Each recording is sent to you and then permanently deleted from my devices.
  • Confidentiality: what you share stays between us. I only disclose information with your explicit consent, or where there is a serious and immediate risk to your safety or someone else's, or where the law requires it.
  • Retention: enquiries that do not become a booking are deleted within 12 months; client records are kept for as long as needed to account for the work and then deleted.
  • Your rights: you can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to its use, at any time through the contact page. You also have the right to complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie).
  • This site uses no advertising or analytics cookies.

3. Bookings, payments, cancellations and refunds

  • Prices are listed on the services page and confirmed when the session is booked. Payment is due at or before the session, as agreed when booking.
  • Rescheduling or cancelling: please give at least 24 hours' notice and the session is moved or refunded in full. With less than 24 hours' notice, or if you do not attend, the session may be charged in full.
  • If I have to cancel, you choose between a new date and a full refund.
  • Journey of Reconnection & Expansion programme: the payment schedule is agreed in writing after the free discovery call and before the first session. If you withdraw during the programme, sessions already held are charged at the individual session rate and the remainder is refunded.
  • The free discovery call carries no obligation.

4. Health, safety and suitability

  • Please tell me, before the first session, about any medical or psychiatric condition, medication, pregnancy, epilepsy, heart condition, recent surgery or current psychological treatment. This is what lets me adapt the work or advise you to wait.
  • Sound baths and drumming involve sustained sound and lying down; shamanic work involves altered states of consciousness reached through drum, rattle, breath and ceremony. If you are in acute emotional distress, under the effect of psychoactive substances or in psychiatric care, tell me in advance so the process can be adapted or postponed.
  • Plant medicines used in the programme are of traditional, ceremonial origin and are used in a spiritual, not medical, context, always with informed consent and after reviewing your allergies and medications.
  • Sessions take place in a prepared, private space with time for grounding and integration at the end. In-person sessions follow basic hygiene and safety practices; you will be told the exact address when the session is confirmed.
  • If you are in crisis, please contact emergency services (112 or 999 in Ireland) or a crisis line such as Samaritans (116 123). These sessions are not an emergency service.

5. Complaints

  • If something in our work did not feel right, please tell me first, through the contact page or directly in a session. I acknowledge complaints within 7 days and give a full answer within 28 days.
  • If you feel the matter is not resolved, you may take it to the professional body I belong to or, for consumer matters, to the Competition and Consumer Protection Commission (ccpc.ie).
  • Complaints are handled confidentially and are never held against you.

Questions about any of this? Get in touch before booking.
